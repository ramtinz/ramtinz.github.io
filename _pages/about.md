<h2 id="about">Research foundations. Industry experience.</h2>



My background is in biomedical engineering, with a PhD from Aalborg University. During my postdoctoral work at the Center of Excellence for Health, Immunity and Infections (CHIP) at Rigshospitalet, I worked on personalized medicine, infectious diseases, and the human gut microbiome, including predictive models for clinical outcomes and SHAP-based explanations.

In industry, I have developed and maintained AI systems in regulated environments, focusing on scalable pipelines, monitoring, and reproducibility. This experience shapes how I assess new AI capabilities: through their reliability, transparency, and usefulness in practice.

<h2 id="contact">Advisory roles & new ventures</h2>

Alongside my technical work, I have contributed to open-source projects, published and reviewed scientific research, and advised and supervised researchers and technical specialists, including data scientists.

I am open to board, scientific committee, and technical advisory roles where my experience could be useful and where I can also learn from others. I value work that addresses a real need and has the potential to make a lasting contribution.

I would also welcome conversations about co-founding a venture with complementary partners. For me, that starts with a worthwhile problem, a sound idea, and people willing to test it carefully and build something useful together.
